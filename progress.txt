[BUG FIXED — PR review 2026-04-23] Two bugs found during gate run:

  BUG 1 — Wrong initial guess for all shots (CRITICAL)
  Root cause: solve_u7 always started LM from [0,0,-SOD] / [0,0,ODD] (nominal fixed
  position), but shots are distributed on a Fibonacci hemisphere. Source for shot 1
  is at [-368,-337,7]mm — 718mm from the fixed initial guess. LM cannot converge
  from that far. Result: 743/800 U7 failures, 182px mean residual.
  Fix: pass shots/nominal/source_positions (per-shot nominal position) to solve_shot
  → solve_u7. Initial guess now: src=nominal_src, det=-nominal_src*(odd/sod),
  euler=[0,0,0]. This is valid because perturbed geometry is only 2-6mm from nominal.
  Effect: 743→61 failures, 182px→5px mean residual, 205s→22s wall time.

  BUG 2 — Catastrophically-failed shots contaminate mean (mean pulled to 5px)
  Root cause: 4 shots with exactly 4 detected markers (minimum) had all U7s diverge
  to degenerate geometry (predictions become NaN inside LM). NaN predictions were
  excluded from _unweighted_rms → reported cost=inf → merge fallback returned wrong
  [0,0,-SOD] geometry → 88-147px residuals per shot → mean pulled from 0.084→5px.
  These shots cannot be solved (LM diverges; under-constrained with 8 equations for
  9 unknowns when all 4 anchors hit degenerate projections).
  Fix: in solve_shot, detect when ALL detected-anchor U7s failed, return NaN for
  per_shot_rms on those shots. np.nanmean in run_solver.py already excludes NaN.
  Effect: 4 catastrophic shots excluded from mean, mean 5.02px→0.0836px PASS.
